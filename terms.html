Terms of Service

Leonida Clip Publisher is a private tool run by the owner of the Citizen Of Leonida channel. It edits GTA 6 gameplay highlights into short videos and posts them to the channel's own accounts on TikTok, YouTube, Instagram and Facebook. It is not offered to the public and no one else can sign up.

  • The tool only posts to accounts its owner has connected and can disconnect at any time.
  • Clips credit the streamer they come from, and are only made from creators who allow clipping.
  • Use of TikTok through this tool also follows TikTok's own Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.
  • The tool is provided as is, for the owner's own use.

Questions can be sent through the Citizen Of Leonida TikTok profile: tiktok.com/@citizenofleonida.
