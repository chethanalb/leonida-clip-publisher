Leonida Clip Publisher

The private tool that edits and posts the Citizen Of Leonida channel's GTA 6 clips.

HomeTerms of ServicePrivacy Policy

Leonida Clip Publisher is a desktop dashboard run by the owner of the Citizen Of Leonida channel. It records GTA 6 gameplay from streamers who allow clipping, cuts the best moments into short vertical videos with a title, cover and captions, and posts them to the channel's own accounts on TikTok, YouTube, Instagram and Facebook.

How it works with TikTok

  • The owner connects the channel's TikTok account with "Login with TikTok" and can disconnect it at any time.
  • Finished clips are sent to the TikTok account with their caption and hashtags, using TikTok's Content Posting API.
  • Every clip credits the streamer it comes from.

It is a private tool: it only posts to the owner's own accounts and nobody else can sign up.
