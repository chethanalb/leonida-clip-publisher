Privacy Policy

The tool runs on the owner's own computer. It stores only what it needs to post videos:

  • Sign-in tokens for the connected TikTok, YouTube and Meta accounts, kept in a private file on that computer and used only to upload videos and read their view counts.
  • The connected TikTok account's display name, shown on the tool's own dashboard.

It does not collect data about other TikTok users, does not sell or share data with anyone, and does not use data for advertising. Disconnecting an account in the tool deletes its stored tokens; access can also be removed at any time in TikTok's settings under Security, Apps and services.

Questions about this policy can be sent through the Citizen Of Leonida TikTok profile: tiktok.com/@citizenofleonida.
